Agrovet Ltd Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2024 • Compliant with the Kenya Data Protection Act (DPA), 2019.

1. Information We Collect

To fulfill your agricultural orders and courier delivery across Kenya, Agrovet Ltd collects:

  • Customer Name and Contact Telephone Number (Safaricom M-Pesa line)
  • Delivery County, Town, and physical farm address or parcel drop point
  • M-Pesa transaction reference codes for automated payment verification
  • Inquiry communications sent via phone, website contact form, or WhatsApp

2. Lawful Purpose of Data Processing

Your personal data is processed solely for order fulfillment, courier parcel booking, automated SMS/WhatsApp dispatch updates, and agronomist follow-up advisory. We do not sell, rent, or lease farmer customer records to third-party marketing companies.

3. Data Security & Storage

Payment information is processed via Safaricom Daraja API encrypted endpoints. Agrovet Ltd never stores your secret M-Pesa PIN numbers. All website communication is secured via 256-bit SSL encryption.

4. Your Rights Under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019

In accordance with Part IV of the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated customer records
  • Request permanent deletion of your customer profile ("Right to be Forgotten")
  • Withdraw consent for promotional SMS marketing at any time

5. Contact Our Data Protection Officer

For data inquiries or deletion requests, email our privacy desk at jjjanuaries@gmail.com or write to Agrovet Ltd, Kirinyaga Road, Agricultural Center, Shop No. 12, Nairobi, Kenya.
