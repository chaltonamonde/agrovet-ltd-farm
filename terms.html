LEGAL & REGULATORY DECLARATION

Agrovet Ltd Terms of Service

Effective Date: October 2024 • Governing Agricultural Commerce, Agrochemical Handling, and Nationwide Logistics in the Republic of Kenya.

Notice to Farmers & Agribusinesses: By placing an order, browsing our catalogue, or initiating an M-Pesa transaction on agrovet.ltd, you enter into a legally binding agreement with Agrovet Ltd and confirm compliance with Kenyan agricultural statutes.

1. Statutory Jurisdiction & Regulatory Compliance

Agrovet Ltd operates as a licensed commercial supplier of agricultural inputs, crop protection chemicals, certified seeds, and spraying equipment from its distribution center at Kirinyaga Road, Nairobi, Kenya. Our operations strictly adhere to:

  • Pest Control Products Act (Cap 346): All insecticides, fungicides, herbicides, and rodenticides sold are registered by the Pest Control Products Board (PCPB).
  • Seeds and Plant Varieties Act (Cap 326): All seed stocks are KEPHIS-certified with verifiable national seal numbers and lot codes.
  • Fertilizers and Animal Foodstuffs Act (Cap 345): Ensuring mineral, granular, and water-soluble fertilizers comply with national nutrient specification standards.

2. Farmer Duty of Care & Safe Agrochemical Handling

Mandatory Safety Condition: Agrochemicals must be handled with utmost diligence. Buyers must strictly observe the following statutory safety protocols:
  • Label Instructions: The buyer is legally obligated to read, comprehend, and observe all product manufacturer dosage rates, dilution ratios, target pest applications, and Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI) printed on the product label.
  • Personal Protective Equipment (PPE): Appropriate safety gear — chemical respirators, splash goggles, nitrile gauntlets, and chemical coveralls — must be donned during mixing and spraying operations.
  • Storage & Custody: Agrochemicals must be stored securely locked away from children, livestock, pets, food, and water sources.
  • Disposal: Chemical packaging and empty containers must be triple-rinsed and punctured before authorized disposal. Burning or washing empty containers near rivers, dams, or boreholes is strictly prohibited under Kenyan environmental law.

3. 100% Genuine Input Guarantee & Anti-Counterfeit Policy

Agrovet Ltd maintains direct dealership agreements with accredited manufacturers and primary importers (e.g., Bayer, Syngenta, Twiga Chemicals, Osho, Simlaw Seeds, Kenya Seed Company). We guarantee that every product dispatched is 100% genuine, unadulterated, and within its declared shelf-life.

Any product showing a compromised tamper-evident seal or damaged hologram at the time of delivery will be replaced immediately under our Anti-Counterfeit Guarantee.

4. Pricing, Currency & Payment Security

  • All prices displayed on agrovet.ltd are quoted in Kenya Shillings (KSh / KES) and include applicable statutory Value Added Tax (VAT) where required by Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) regulations.
  • Payments are processed securely via Safaricom M-Pesa (Lipa na M-Pesa Till / Paybill) or electronic bank transfer. We never request or store your private 4-digit M-Pesa PIN.
  • Orders are confirmed for courier packing only upon receipt of a valid M-Pesa transaction confirmation code.

5. Nationwide Delivery Across 47 Counties

We partner with licensed national courier parcel services (including Fargo Courier, Wells Fargo, Easy Coach Parcel, North Rift, Guardian, and 2NK) to provide door-to-door or parcel-office collection across all 47 counties in Kenya.

  • Dispatch Timelines: Orders finalized before 2:00 PM (Mon–Fri) are booked for same-day dispatch from Nairobi. Standard transit duration is 24 to 48 hours depending on destination county.
  • Parcel Inspection Duty: Farmers are required to inspect the tamper-evident exterior tape and package integrity at the courier office before signing the waybill receipt.
  • Perishable / Bulky Inputs: Heavy bulk fertilizer (50kg bags) and seedling trays may require dedicated regional freight arrangements coordinated directly with our logistics desk.

6. Returns, Refunds & Cancellation Policy

Due to the sensitive nature of biological inputs and regulated chemical formulations, our return policy adheres to strict safety parameters:

  • Non-Returnable Items: Agrochemical bottles with opened induction seals, seed packets with broken KEPHIS tamper seals, and custom foliar mixes cannot be returned under any circumstances to prevent contamination and adulteration risks.
  • Damaged or Wrongly Dispatched Items: If an incorrect product is delivered, report the discrepancy within 48 hours of courier collection via WhatsApp or phone. We will arrange a courier exchange at our expense.
  • Hardware & Sprayer Warranty: Knapsack sprayers, motorized mist-blowers, and brass lances carry a 6-month limited manufacturer warranty covering manufacturing defects. Breakage caused by dropping, chemical crystallization from lack of flushing, or user misuse is excluded.

7. Limitation of Agricultural Yield Liability

Agricultural productivity is inherently influenced by biological variables outside human control. Agrovet Ltd warrants the authenticity and standard quality of inputs supplied, but cannot warrant final crop yield or harvest outcome.

To the maximum extent permitted by Kenyan law, Agrovet Ltd is not liable for crop loss resulting from:

  • Adverse climatic events (drought, flooding, frost, hailstorms).
  • Soil fertility deficits, incorrect pH, or unrectified salinity.
  • Off-label pesticide mixing, overdose, or application during extreme midday heat.
  • Pest resistance arising from repeated, unrotated chemical mode-of-action use.
