ABOUT AGROVET LTD

Empowering Kenyan Farmers with Genuine Inputs

Agrovet Ltd (agrovet.ltd) was founded with a clear mission: to protect Kenyan farmers from counterfeit agrochemicals and low-germination seeds by providing a reliable, 100% genuine e-commerce supply chain across all 47 counties.

PCPB & KEPHIS Registered

We hold valid dealer licensing under the Pest Control Products Board (PCPB Commercial Dealer Licence & KEPHIS Certification). Every crop protection chemical in our inventory is sourced exclusively from registered multinational and local manufacturers.

47-County Courier Logistics

Whether you are farming in Nyandarua, Trans Nzoia, Meru, or Kilifi, we get your inputs to you within 24 to 48 hours via trusted courier parcel networks and secure packaging.

Licensed Field Agronomists

We don't just sell chemicals; we help you solve farm problems. Our agronomists provide personalized advice on pest management, knapsack calibration, and crop nutrition.

VISIT OUR NAIROBI STORE

Physical Shop & Collection Center

Farmers are welcome to visit our walk-in shop to consult our agronomists in person or pick up orders placed online for zero delivery charge.
