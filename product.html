Application & Knapsack Directions

Mix according to prescribed knapsack ratios using clean pond or borehole water.

Best Practice Spraying Guidelines:

  • Spray early in the morning (6:00 AM – 9:00 AM) or late afternoon to minimize evaporation.
  • Avoid spraying when rainfall is expected within 4 hours.
  • Use flat-fan nozzles for soil herbicides and hollow-cone nozzles for contact insecticides.
