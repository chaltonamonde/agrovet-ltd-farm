Return & Refund Policy

1. 7-Day Return Window: If you receive an incorrect product or a sealed container with factory defects, you may return it within 7 days of receipt for an immediate replacement or full refund.

2. Agrochemical Safety Regulation: In accordance with PCPB safety regulations, opened chemical bottles or unsealed seed packets cannot be accepted back for returns once unsealed to prevent contamination.

3. Damaged Parcel Claims: If your courier parcel shows external damage or spillage upon delivery, report it to the courier agent and notify us on WhatsApp (+254 700 000 000) within 24 hours with photos for a free replacement dispatch.

4. Refund Processing: Approved refunds are credited directly to your original Safaricom M-Pesa account within 24 business hours.
