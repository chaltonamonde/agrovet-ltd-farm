## 7. Photo Supply Action List for Store Owner

When replacing specimen illustrations with final retail photographs, the store owner must follow these rules:
1. **Lighting & Background:** Shoot in natural diffuse daylight against a plain light neutral or cream surface.
2. **Angle:** Straight-on front view with the PCPB registration label and active ingredient text clearly legible.
3. **Dimensions:** Square 1200 × 1200 px (minimum 800 × 800 px), single product per photo.
4. **Format:** WebP format, target file size under 150 KB.
