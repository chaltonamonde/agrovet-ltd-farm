This document records the provenance, licensing, and photographic attributions for all media assets utilized across the Agrovet Ltd platform.

## 1. Licensing Principles & Compliance

In strict adherence to project guidelines:
* **Zero Competitor or Hotlinked Images:** No assets have been scraped or hotlinked from competitor e-commerce sites or Google Images.
* **Commercial Use Rights:** Non-product photographic assets are derived exclusively from verified free-to-use commercial licensing repositories (Unsplash, Pexels, Wikimedia Commons Open Agricultural Archives, and Agrovet Ltd in-house technical field photography).
* **Kenyan Agricultural Context:** All human subjects depicted represent African smallholder farmers operating in authentic Kenyan field conditions (Nairobi, Rift Valley, Central Kenya, Western Kenya).
* **Chemical Safety & Product Accuracy:** Product pack images accurately depict the chemical formulations, active ingredients, and PCPB registration placeholders defined in the catalogue, preventing dangerous misidentification.

## 3. Product Pack Photography Guidelines for Store Owner

To replace specimen illustrations with high-resolution retail photos taken at the Nairobi shop:
1. **Lighting:** Utilize indirect morning daylight. Avoid harsh shadows or direct flash reflections on plastic bottles.
2. **Background:** Position products on a light neutral, matte cream surface.
3. **Framing:** Place the product in the exact center of a square 1200 × 1200 px crop.
4. **Legibility:** Ensure the front label, brand name, active ingredient, and PCPB registration seal are in crisp focus.
5. **Post-Processing:** Save as `.webp` at 80%–85% quality (file size $< 150\text{ KB}$).
